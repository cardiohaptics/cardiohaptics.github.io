Privacy Policy

The short version: the app keeps everything on your phone.

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no password.
  • The app does not connect to the internet, so nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app asks for no system permissions — not your microphone, camera, location, contacts or health data.
  • Your settings stay on your phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

In detail

What the app stores on your phone

mrmr: cardio haptics saves a small amount of information in the app's own storage on your device so that it behaves the way you left it:

  • your settings, such as whether sound and vibration are on and whether stethoscope mode is in use;
  • your vibration strength setting, which suits the vibration to your particular iPhone;
  • your practice and test preferences, such as how long a test should be;
  • whether you have accepted the in-app disclaimer.

This information never leaves your phone by itself. We cannot see it, and there is no copy of it on any server.

What we do not collect

We do not collect personal information through the app. Specifically, the app contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not ask for any system permission. The app's privacy manifest, which Apple publishes on the App Store listing, declares no tracking and no collected data types.

Links out of the app

Each finding lists the published sources it was built from, and the disclaimer screen links to this policy. Tapping one of those opens the page in Safari, which is an ordinary visit to that website and is handled under its own privacy practices. The app itself makes no network request, sends nothing with the link, and is not told whether you followed it.

If you email us

When you write to cardiohaptics@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you choose to include, such as your iPhone model or a screenshot. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. The address is a Gmail account, so Google handles the mail as our email provider. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your message to anyone else.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. As with any web host, GitHub's servers may record ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to serve the page. That is handled under GitHub's own privacy practices and we do not receive it.

The App Store

Apple may give us aggregate statistics about the app, such as how many people downloaded it or how often it crashed, for users who have chosen to share that information with developers in their iPhone settings. These figures are summarised by Apple and do not identify you to us.

Children

The app is intended for people learning cardiac examination. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

How long things are kept, and how to have them deleted

On your phone. The settings the app stores stay on your device until you change them or delete the app. Deleting the app removes them, and no copy of them ever reaches us, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Email you send us. We keep correspondence only as long as we need it to answer you and to fix what you reported, and in any case no longer than twelve months, after which it is deleted. If you would like it deleted sooner, email us and ask; we will do it and confirm, within thirty days at the latest.

Withdrawing consent. The only thing you are asked to agree to is the disclaimer shown when the app first opens, and your answer is stored as a single yes on your own phone. Deleting the app withdraws it. Because the app collects nothing, sends nothing and asks for no system permission, there is no other consent to withdraw and nothing that withdrawing it would stop.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. The history of every change is public in the repository that publishes this site.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else, go to cardiohaptics@gmail.com.
