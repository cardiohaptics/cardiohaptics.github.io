Frequently asked questions

How do I use the app?

Open the app and follow the on-screen instructions. The app is made to be used together with a stethoscope, so that you can hear the heart sounds and feel them through your phone at the same time. Use headphones or keep the room quiet for the clearest listening.

What should I do if I cannot hear or feel the heart sounds?

If you cannot hear anything, check that:

  • your iPhone is not in silent mode and the volume is turned up;
  • audio is not being sent to another device over Bluetooth or AirPlay;
  • any headphones or stethoscope are fully and correctly connected.

If you cannot feel anything, check that:

  • Settings → Sounds & Haptics has system haptics enabled;
  • your iPhone is not in a case or holder that muffles the vibration;
  • Low Power Mode is off, as it can reduce haptic feedback.

Vibration can also feel noticeably stronger or weaker from one iPhone model to the next. If it still does not work after these checks, close and reopen the app, and then email us so we can help.

How do I report a problem?

Email cardiohaptics@gmail.com with a description of the problem, the steps that lead to it, your iPhone model and your iOS version. Screenshots or a screen recording are very welcome and usually make the fix much faster.

How can I provide feedback?

We read every message. Send suggestions, requests and general comments to cardiohaptics@gmail.com. If you are using the app for teaching or study, telling us how you use it is especially helpful.
